Help

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know, in one place.

Earning points

How do I earn Finch Points?

Make an eligible purchase at a participating store, either directly or through your recommendation. Eligible purchases earn Finch Points that contribute toward your rewards.

How do I know if a purchase is eligible?

Eligibility depends on each partner's rules, which can exclude certain products or categories or set a minimum bill. Check the offer details or ask at the outlet.

Why are my points showing as pending?

Some partners confirm purchases later. Pending points become available once confirmed.

Where can I see my Finch Points?

In the Finch app wallet, along with your pending points, history and tier progress.

Redeeming

What can I redeem my Finch Points for?

Bill savings at eligible outlets, gift vouchers and gift items, subject to programme rules.

Can I use only some of my points?

Yes. Partial and full redemption are supported, subject to limits.

What if my bill is cancelled?

Points held for that bill are released back to your wallet.

Do my vouchers expire?

Yes. Vouchers are time-bound, and the validity is shown on each voucher.

Account and tiers

How do I join Finch?

Sign up in the Finch app or on the website, scan a QR code, or ask at a participating outlet. You verify your mobile number with a one-time password and receive your Finch Member ID.

Do I have to share my birthday or email?

No. Email is optional, and your date of birth is only needed if you opt in to birthday benefits.

Can I choose which messages I receive?

Yes. Marketing consent is separate from service messages, and you can change your preferences at any time.

Can I track my reward history?

Yes. The app shows your full history by partner, outlet, date and status.

How do I move up a tier?

Tiers are based on qualifying activity, such as annual eligible spend. Your progress is shown in the app.

Do my points expire?

Yes, by batch, according to programme rules. We aim to remind you before they do.

What happens to my points if I return an item?

The matching points are reversed based on the original earning rule.

How do I manage marketing messages?

Update your communication preferences in the app at any time.

For businesses

How do we join Finch?

Send an enquiry through our contact page and our team will take you through onboarding.

Will Finch work with our POS?

Finch integrates through standard APIs. Tell us your POS or ERP provider and we will confirm the approach.

Can each outlet have different rules?

Yes. Rules can be set by programme, partner, outlet, category, product, tier and campaign.
