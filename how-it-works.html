How it works

From your bill to your next reward.

A clear path from the counter to the reward, with every point accounted for.

Earning

Earn at the counter

Join once

Sign up through the app, website, a partner counter or a QR code, and verify your mobile number by OTP. You receive one unique Finch Member ID.

Identify yourself

Share your mobile number, Member ID or QR code when you pay.

We validate the purchase

Finch checks the member, partner, outlet, invoice and eligible items.

Points are calculated

Base earning, your tier multiplier and any eligible campaign bonus are applied.

Your wallet updates

The points are recorded, and you receive a notification.

Redeeming

Two ways to redeem.

At the counter

Choose how many points to use. They are held while you check out and deducted only once your bill is confirmed. If the bill is cancelled, they return to you.

In the app

Exchange points for a unique, time-bound voucher or QR code, then present it at the outlet.

Good to know

The details, plainly.

Pending points

Some partners confirm purchases later, so points may show as pending before they can be spent.

Returns

If you return an item, the matching points are reversed based on the original earning rule.

Expiry

Points expire in batches. We plan to remind you ahead of time, for example 30, 7 and 1 day before.

Oldest points first

Unless programme rules say otherwise, your oldest eligible points are used first.

Redemption limits

Partners may set minimum and maximum limits, and exclude certain products.
